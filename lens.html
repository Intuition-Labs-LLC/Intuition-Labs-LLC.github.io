Drop a lens. Get a verdict, with a receipt.

Drop one of Neuronpedia's pre-fitted Jacobian-lens files (huggingface.co/neuronpedia/jacobian-lens, the raw *_jacobian_lens.pt) — or any plain-JSON lens {d_model, vectors: number[][]}. This page fits held-out lens directions into sparse nonnegative cones, compares two layers with Δ_μ, gates each fit at a disclosed tolerance, and hands you the receipt.

Everything runs in this tab. No request leaves it — check the network panel. Same file, same numbers, every time: the pipeline has no randomness anywhere.

    What this is not: this page does not run a model, does not fit lenses, and cannot tell you
    what a lens direction means. It stores, fits, compares, and gates the objects other
    tools produce. A high escalation rate is the honest reading — a k=3 nonnegative cone of 48
    lens rows explains only a small slice of a held-out direction, and the gate says so instead
    of pretending. The tolerance (0.85 relative residual) is page-local and printed on the
    receipt it gates.
